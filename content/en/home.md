# Agentic AI engineering, from prototype to production.

A small senior team that builds AI agents and AI features into real products. Few projects at a time, full attention on each one.

[Talk to us](/contact)

## What we do

### Agentic development for your team
We set up coding agents and automated review and testing in your team's workflow, and train your engineers to work with them.

### An AI feature in your app
We design, build and ship one AI feature to production in your web or mobile app, with evals, monitoring and cost controls.

### Fractional forward deployed engineer
A senior engineer embedded with your team to take AI from idea to production, for companies without an AI team of their own.

[See the services](/services)

## How we work

- **One clear deliverable.** Scope is written and agreed before we start. Anything else is a second phase.
- **Small pilots first.** Four to six weeks to something real, then you decide what comes next.
- **Senior engineers on your project.** And QA on every delivery.
- **Measured, not hoped.** Evals and tracing from the first week, so you can see what the AI gets right and what it gets wrong.
