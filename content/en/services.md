# Services

Every engagement starts with a short conversation about the problem, not the technology. If AI is not the right answer, we will say so.

## Agentic development for your team

For web and mobile teams of 5 to 30 engineers. About 4 weeks.

- Coding agents set up in your repos, with the rules and context your codebase needs.
- Automated code review and test generation in your existing workflow.
- Hands-on training so your engineers keep using it after we leave.

## An AI feature in your app

For companies with a product that want one AI feature in production. About 4 to 6 weeks.

- One feature, from design to production: an assistant, a document extractor, a smart search, a support agent.
- Web, Android, iOS or React Native.
- Evals, monitoring and cost controls included, so the feature can be trusted and maintained.

## Fractional forward deployed engineer

For companies adopting AI without an AI team of their own. Monthly.

- A senior engineer who works inside your team, with your people and your data.
- Finds where AI pays off, builds it, and puts it in production.
- Leaves your team able to run and improve what was built.
