# About Prototype

Prototype is an engineering boutique from El Salvador. We build agentic AI and AI features for companies in El Salvador and abroad.

We stay small on purpose. We take a few projects at a time so each one gets senior attention from start to finish, and no one learns on your budget.

Founded by an engineer with over a decade shipping apps for global companies.

## What we believe

- AI is worth it only when it solves a real problem better than the alternative.
- A pilot should end in something running, not a slide deck.
- If you cannot measure it, you cannot trust it.
- Your team should understand what we built and be able to keep it running.
