# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-17

This policy explains how Prototype ("we", "us") handles personal data on this website and in the software services we build and operate for our business clients, including services that use the WhatsApp Business Platform.

## Who we are

Prototype is an engineering company based in El Salvador. For any privacy question or request, write to icarloscornejo@outlook.com.

## This website

- This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts.
- If you email us, we receive your name, email address and whatever you write. We use it only to reply to you and to talk about working together.
- We keep those emails while the conversation is useful, and delete them when you ask us to.

## Services we provide to our clients

We build and operate software for businesses, such as assistants that answer their customers on WhatsApp. When we do, the business is responsible for the personal data of its customers, and we process that data only on its behalf and following its instructions.

- **What data:** messages, phone numbers, names and other information that people share when they contact one of our clients, plus the business information our client gives us to run the service.
- **What for:** only to provide the service our client hired us for, for example answering a question or preparing a quote.
- **What we never do:** we do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models.

## Who else is involved

To run these services we rely on providers that process data for us under their own terms: Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform), cloud hosting and database providers, and AI model providers. We share with them only what each one needs to do its part. We can tell you which providers a given service uses if you write to us.

## Security

Access to personal data is limited to the people working on the service. Data is encrypted in transit, and access keys are kept out of the source code.

## How long we keep data

We keep personal data only as long as the service needs it or the law requires it, and then delete it.

## Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data. If the data was processed for one of our clients, we will pass your request to that client and act on its instructions. To delete data, follow the steps on our [Data Deletion](/data-deletion) page.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
