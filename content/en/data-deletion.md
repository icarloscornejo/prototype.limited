# Data Deletion

You can ask us to delete the personal data we hold about you, including messages you sent to a business that uses a service built by Prototype, such as a WhatsApp assistant.

## How to ask

1. Email icarloscornejo@outlook.com with the subject "Data deletion request".
2. Include the phone number or email you used, and the business you contacted, if any.
3. We will confirm that we received your request and tell you when the data has been deleted.

If the data belongs to one of our clients, we will forward your request to that client and delete the data as it instructs, unless the law requires us to keep it.
